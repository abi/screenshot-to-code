Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WhimsyWorks, Inc. (“we” or “us”) handles your information when you use Screenshot to Code.

1. What We Collect

  1. Account info: your name, email address, and profile picture, including from Google if you sign in with Google.
  2. Your content: the screenshots, prompts, and other files you upload, and the code we generate.
  3. Payments: purchase and subscription details. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your full card number.
  4. Usage data: how you use the app, device and browser info, and error logs.

2. How We Use It

We use your information to run the service, generate code from your uploads, manage your account and payments, provide support, and keep the service secure and working well.

3. Who We Share It With

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run Screenshot to Code, such as AI model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google) that generate code from your uploads, and providers for sign-in, payments, hosting, analytics, and support. We may also share information when required by law.

4. Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address, and profile picture. We use them only to create your account and sign you in. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information, or delete your account, by emailing support@getwhimsyworks.com. The service is not intended for anyone under 18.
